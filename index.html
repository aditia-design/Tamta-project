The Coppersmiths of Almora

For generations, the Tamta families of Almora have turned flat sheets of copper into vessels for water, worship and the home.

The Craft

The name comes from tamra, copper. Every piece is cut, beaten, fired, joined and textured by hand, one vessel at a time.

The City

The craft grew up alongside the hill town. Its temples, rituals and kitchens created the demand, and the sound of hammering became part of Almora's lanes.
